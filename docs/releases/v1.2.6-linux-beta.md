Prefer a terminal, or the AppImage won't start? Use [`Pokopia-Native-for-Linux-beta.zip`](https://github.com/xFractals/pokopia-native/releases/download/v1.2.6-linux-beta/Pokopia-Native-for-Linux-beta.zip) instead.

For **x86-64** Linux PCs and the Steam Deck: a CPU with AVX2 and FMA3 (most from about 2015 on), a Vulkan 1.3 driver (SteamOS has one), about 15 GB free, and your own dump of the game.

## What's new in this beta (built on 1.2.6)

**Join the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Aj9GqxZj8V)**: Linux and Steam Deck reports go to **#linux-beta**.

**This is an open beta, and an early one.** The Linux build has **never run on a real Steam Deck**, nor on a Linux PC with a real graphics card. It was
made and tested only under WSL (Linux inside Windows) with Mesa's software renderer, where it builds the game from a dump and reaches the title
screen in a window; nobody has played further than that on Linux yet. On your computer the first start may well fail. That is fine, and exactly what this beta is for: when it does, **the logs are
what we need** (how to send them is below). Windows and Mac players: this pre-release is not for you; use [1.2.6](https://github.com/xFractals/pokopia-native/releases/latest).

## This version at a glance

- **Devices:** Linux and the Steam Deck (beta).
- **Playing together:** Interact everywhere (experimental).
- **Graphics:** sharp upscaling and display settings (Linux).
- **Controls:** mouse look and controllers (Linux); keyboard rebinding (Linux).
- **Builder and saves:** built from your own dump; bring your save over (Linux); .nup dumps.
- Link Play on Linux joins a host by its address; finding hosts on the same network comes later.
