## What's new in Android beta 2

- **No more device-test box when the game starts.** The game app now says plainly what to do if its files aren't on the phone yet ("on your
  PC, press Install on my device"), and the install no longer shows anything on the phone while it prepares the app's folder. (Our private
  tester's finding: thank you.)
- **The device test's result is a clear verdict**: "This phone can run Pokopia Native" and what to do next, or why not.
- **The first start shows the game.** Its first screens use graphics the game makes while it runs, which only your PC can prepare: the
  screen stayed black behind the touch controls until a refresh. The first install now opens the game on the phone for a minute or two,
  prepares those graphics and copies them over. (Found in our last check before this beta.)
- **Less work every frame on a phone held sideways**: the game set up its link to the screen again twice a frame; now only when something
  changes. (Found in our last check before this beta.)
- **An interrupted install is safe**: the game's files count as installed only once they're all there; pressing Install again copies only
  what's missing.
- **Installing again after an uninstall works.** Android leaves the old app's files behind, which the new one couldn't use (it would have
  started without its logs or save); Install now clears them and copies the game's files again. (Found in our last check before this beta.)
- **The builder's phone steps have progress pages**, and Platform-Tools are found when unzipped next to the builder.
- **The guide was rewritten** for players: whether your phone qualifies, each brand's menus for USB debugging, and answers to the usual
  problems.

### What's in it

- **The builder's Android steps**: **Install on my device**, **Refresh the graphics** and **Make a report**, each with its own progress page
  and plain-words messages for the usual problems (no phone found, the phone asking to allow this PC, Xiaomi's "Install via USB", Play Protect,
  not enough space).
- **Touch controls**: both sticks, the D-pad, A B X Y, L R ZL ZR, − and +, and a menu button. They hide while a controller is connected (a
  handheld's own buttons count, so a handheld never shows them). A finger anywhere else touches the game itself.
- **Landscape, full screen, sound.** **Back** opens the settings (resolution, frame rate, controls). **Leaving the app pauses the game**,
  coming back carries on. **Your character's name** gets a box with your phone's keyboard.
- **Refresh the graphics**: the game makes some graphics while you play, which the phone can't prepare itself, so they can be missing the
  first time. Your PC prepares them and copies over only the new ones.
- **Make a report**: one file with the game's logs and facts about your phone, never your save or the game's files.

## This version at a glance

- **Built on 1.3.3**, with the Windows game's recompiler and Vulkan renderer, for arm64 phones and handhelds.
- **Controls:** touch controls on the screen, or a Bluetooth or USB controller, or a handheld's own buttons.
- **Settings:** Back opens them (resolution, frame rate, controls).
- **Not in this beta yet:** Link Play, and bringing a save over.

<details>
<summary><b>Getting started</b></summary>

The full guide, with each phone brand's menus and screenshots, is **`docs/ANDROID.md`** in the zip. In short:

1. **Check your phone** with the device test (optional): it says whether the phone can run the game.
2. **Unzip** the download into a folder of its own and double-click **Pokopia Native Builder.cmd**. Pick **Play on your Android phone or
   handheld**; the **Your PC** page checks what's needed: Python, .NET 10, Git, Python's `cryptography` add-on and Google's
   [Platform-Tools](https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools) (unzip them into the same folder).
3. **Build** from your dump (the first build takes 30 to 60 minutes).
4. On the phone, **turn on USB debugging** once (the guide shows where for Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola and handhelds).
5. Connect the phone by USB, unlock it, and press **Install on my device**. The first time copies about 8 GB, then opens the game on the phone
   for a minute or two to prepare the graphics of its first screens: leave the phone be until the builder says it's done.
6. Open **Pokopia Native** on the phone. The first start takes a few minutes.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What's tested, and what isn't</b></summary>

**Tested** in Android's emulator (Android 16) on a Windows PC, from a freshly unzipped download into a new build folder: Setup and the PC
check; the build from a 2.0.1 dump; the install on a phone that had the device test (it was replaced, and nothing showed on the phone while
the install prepared the app's folder); the app's messages when the game's files are missing or incomplete; a first install on an empty phone,
which prepared the first graphics by itself; the first start, straight to the language choice with nothing missing; the on-screen A choosing
English and the on-screen Up and A answering Yes; the game saving; Back opening and closing the settings; Home and coming back; the sound;
installing again after an update (only what changed was copied) and after an uninstall (the leftovers cleared, the game's files copied
again); the graphics refresh (the first install runs it); and the report (it holds no save, game files or pictures, and no names from the
PC). The device test's verdict on the emulator, and on a private tester's Snapdragon 8 Elite phone (beta 1's version). The Windows game's
tests pass.

**Not tested:** playing on a real phone or handheld for any length of time, any real Adreno or Mali graphics driver in the game itself, the
frame rate and heat on a phone, real controllers and handhelds' own buttons, the name box inside the game, Link Play from Android, and anything
past the first screens (the emulator runs out of memory on our PC soon after).

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Known issues</b></summary>

- **Snapdragon 865 and older can't run this beta**, and Pixel, Exynos and Dimensity phones are untested (see above).
- **New places** can show missing graphics the first time, until **Refresh the graphics** has run.
- **Quitting:** swipe the app away in your recent apps. The game saves by itself, as on the Switch.
- **Updating later** needs the same build folder (it holds the app's signing key). Without it the app has to be uninstalled first, which
  deletes its save.
- **Memory:** on 8 GB phones Android may close the game if other apps hold a lot of memory; close them first, and say so in the report.
- **Bringing your save** from the PC or another phone isn't in this beta yet.
- **DLC** isn't in the Android app yet, and a Windows game made from this zip doesn't load it either: for your DLC on a PC, use 1.4.0's
  Windows download.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Reporting: what helps</b></summary>

1. With the phone connected and unlocked: **Make a report** in the builder. It puts `pokopia-android-report-<date>.zip` in your build folder:
   the game's newest logs, the phone's model, Android version, chip, memory and graphics driver, and the build's logs. It **never** includes your
   save, game files, the app, your signing key, shaders or pictures.
2. Open an issue with the **[Android beta report](https://github.com/xFractals/pokopia-native/issues/new?template=android-beta-report.yml)** form and
   attach the zip, or post it in **#bug-reports** on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/Aj9GqxZj8V).
3. If the device test said the phone can't run it, its report (on your clipboard) is all we need.

</details>
