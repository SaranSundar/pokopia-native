**Pokopia built natively for the Mac and the iPhone from your own dump.**

## What's new in 1.0.0

- Builds Pokémon Pokopia natively from **your own dump**, update 2.0.0 (v327680) or 2.0.1 (v655360).
- **Mac:** the Mac app, and the iPhone app (IPA or an Xcode project), signed with your own paid Apple Developer team.
- **Windows:** builds the iPhone app without a Mac, and signs it with your certificate (.p12) and development profile (`Pokopia Native Builder.cmd`).
- Direct camera look by dragging, even touch timing, and the app icon taken from the title screen.
- The first launch of a fresh build has a black title screen until the shaders the game makes while it runs are recorded and added with `nxs2.py refresh`; see the README.
- No game files or prebuilt apps are included or ever will be.

## This version at a glance

- **Devices:** **new:** Mac and iPhone.
- **Controls:** **new:** drag to look (Mac and iPhone).
- **Builder and saves:** **new:** built from your own dump; **new:** the iPhone app from a Mac or a Windows PC.
